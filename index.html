Translation Chain

Ancient Text Workspace

For scripts with no OCR/translation model at all — cuneiform, the Voynich manuscript, and other undeciphered or untrained scripts — "Analyze symbols" can't translate what no one can read, but it can segment, count, and export the individual glyphs for side-by-side comparison, which is how that research actually gets done. "Attempt AI reading" instead sends the photo to Claude directly and asks it to make an honest, hedged attempt — real script/period identification where possible, and a plain "I can't reliably read the specific signs" where that's the truth, rather than a confident-sounding invented translation.
